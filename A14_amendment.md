### A14 (2026-11-21): objective axis, asset-survival probability (plan written before any A14 code)
Status: plan only. No A14 code exists and no A14 run has been made when this text is committed. The freeze point is the commit that adds this text (workflow of 2026-11-17: no tags).

**A14.1 Why, and what is already known (disclosure).** A1 to A13 scored every method by the expected killed value, and every strong baseline (exact DP, CEP, rollouts) is built for that objective. The conclusion "exact-model planning is within a few percent of the optimum" is therefore a statement about that objective. The defence question is often a probability: does the asset survive, that is, does the leaked value stay below a tolerance? That objective is a probabilistic-goal MDP (Xu and Mannor 2011, IJCAI, read in full on 2026-11-21 [DOC]): the probability that the accumulated reward reaches a target; they show that the optimal policy may depend on the accumulated reward, that randomisation does not help, that the problem is NP-hard in general and solvable in pseudo-polynomial time by state augmentation with the accumulated reward.
Exploratory evidence that motivated A14 (post-hoc, not a result): script explore_objective_mismatch.py, throwaway seed 3, n = 4, D = (5, 10), 40 instances per cell, 8 cells (rho 0.8 and 1.1, s 2 and 4, a 0.5 and 0.9) for each of two thresholds, no confidence intervals. The author re-ran it on the laptop and obtained identical numbers [FILE: console output]. At threshold 75% of the total value, the EV-optimal DP policy lost 1.7% to 21.8% of the optimal survival probability (mean 6.9% over the 8 cells), ROLL_CEP lost 2% to 29%, GMR 25% to 57% and EDF 8% to 72%. At the threshold "kill everything" EDF lost nothing in any cell, while the EV-optimal policy lost 44% on average, in cells with small success probabilities (one cell with U_opt = 0 is undefined). The exploratory instances (seed 3) are not used again.
Expectation stated before the run [OPEN]: objective-aware planning with the exact model (policy improvement under the goal objective) will probably close most of the gap at n = 4, which would give the outcome MISMATCH below and not GO. The audit on seed 1 (A14.7) is exploratory and is read before the main run; it cannot change this plan.

**A14.2 Objective.** Threat i has value v_i in {1, 2, 3}. W = sum of v_i. Leakage tolerance lam in {0.25, 0.5}. The asset survives if the value of the threats that are not killed before breach is at most lam * W, that is, if the killed value reaches need = ceil((1 - lam) * W) (values are integers; a 1e-9 guard is used inside the ceiling). Objective: maximise U = P(killed value >= need). The exact optimum U_opt is a DP over (t, current target, progress per threat, accumulated killed value); it stops as soon as the accumulated value reaches need (success is certain) and returns 0 when the accumulated value plus the value of all still killable threats is below need. The instance, hidden classes, priors, retained progress, slew and breach rules are those of M0 and are not changed.

**A14.3 Design.** Main grid: n = 4; rho in {0.8, 1.1, 1.5}; s in {2, 4}; label accuracy a in {0.5, 0.9}; lam in {0.25, 0.5}; D = (5, 10), values uniform on {1, 2, 3}, u_low 0.35 (as A6 to A13). 24 cells, 200 instances per cell, master seed 20261251; instances are those of make_instance_k for the same arguments, so the two lam cells of a configuration share their instances (paired). Bootstrap seed 20261252 (B = 2000, one generator per cell). Audit: same grid, 20 instances per cell, seed 1. Spot check after the decision (descriptive only, no go rule): n = 5, rho in {0.8, 1.1}, 16 cells, 100 instances, seed 20261253; its cost at n = 5 is not measured.

**A14.4 Methods (17).** EV-based (12): the 11 rules of A13 (FAIR, EDF, LLF, SE, GMR, BI, CEP, LA1, ROLL_CEP, ROLL_GMR, ROLL_EDF), which ignore the accumulated value, and DPEV, the exact optimum of the expected-value objective evaluated on U. Objective-aware (5): CEPS, a certainty-equivalent planner that, among feasible sequences whose value reaches the remaining need, takes the one with the largest minimum slack (ties: earlier finish, larger value), and otherwise the feasible sequence of largest value (then GMR); RU_CEP, RU_GMR, RU_EDF, RU_DPEV, the exact one-step policy improvement of CEP, GMR, EDF and DPEV under the goal objective. The reference is the exact goal optimum U_opt, which is not a competitor.

**A14.5 Metric and flags.** For a cell: rel_m = 1 - sum U_m / sum U_opt and abs_m = (sum U_opt - sum U_m) / N. A cell is informative if 0.10 <= mean U_opt <= 0.99 (a relative loss of a probability near 0 or 1 is not material); other cells are reported and cannot be flagged. For a set of methods the best method (smallest rel) is re-selected in every resample; the one-sided 95% lower bounds are the 5th percentiles of rel and abs. A cell is flagged for a set if it is informative, lo(rel) >= 0.05 and lo(abs) >= 0.02 (the absolute floor of 2 points of survival probability is added because a 5% relative loss of a small probability is not material; the floor is my choice and is fixed here). Point-flagged: the point estimates satisfy both thresholds. Sets: ALL (17 methods) and EV (the 12 EV-based methods). A region is two flagged cells that differ in exactly one factor by one position of the grid order (n, rho, s, a, lam), both with s >= 2 and rho <= 1.5.

**A14.6 Descriptive outputs (no decision).** Mean regret per method over informative cells; share of informative cells in which the best method is objective-aware; share of instances in which the first action of the goal optimum differs from that of DPEV; instance-level mean, 95th percentile and maximum loss of the best method of ALL and of the best EV-based method; Spearman correlation of the demand-bound load with the instance loss of DPEV (the regime-variable check of C2). No control run is defined; the checks are the tests of A14.7.

**A14.9 Decision table (applied by script, first match).**
- GO: a region of cells flagged for ALL. The gap survives the objective-aware baselines. Candidate spine; next steps (author decides): n = 5 spot check, literature check on probabilistic-goal planning and chance-constrained scheduling, frozen confirmatory protocol.
- MISMATCH: no GO, but a region of cells flagged for EV. The objective matters: the best EV-based method loses at least 5% (and 2 points) of the survival probability, while an objective-aware method closes it at n = 4 with the exact model. Meaning: the paper cannot claim that EV planning suffices for survival objectives; the open question moves to scale (n >= 7, decision-time budget, learned versus rollout), which needs a separate plan.
- MARGINAL: neither, but a region of point-flagged cells for ALL. Extend to 1000 instances per cell on the same seed stream (staged extension, disclosed) before any claim.
- NO-GO: otherwise. The objective axis is closed and reported as a negative result; the exploratory evidence of A14.1 is reported as such.

**A14.10 Limits stated now.** n = 4 and an exact model with a known prior: the test cannot show a benefit of learning, only whether the EV-based baselines are objective-mismatched. The two lam values and the form of the goal are my choices; other forms (several asset classes, expected leaked value above a cap) are not tested. Success probability is a different objective from the one of the earlier amendments and the amendments A1 to A13 are post-hoc relative to each other; no multiplicity correction is applied across amendments. The relation to the probabilistic-goal MDP literature is established from Xu and Mannor 2011 only; no directed-energy scheduling paper with this objective was found in a non-systematic search [OPEN].
