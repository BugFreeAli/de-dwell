### A20 (2026-11-27): robustness of the scale-learning planner (plan written before any A20 code is committed)
Status: plan only. The freeze point is the commit that adds this text (workflow of 2026-11-17: no tags). No A20 run has been made on the author's machine when this text is committed.

**A20.1 Why, and what is already known (disclosure).** A19 (outcome WORKS, second label LEARNING-ADDS) showed that a planner that learns the dwell scale (ADAPT) recovers essentially the whole gap to the informed rollout planner at n = 5 and 7, and that it is 3.5 to 15.6 points above CLAMP (no abandoning, no learning). Four weaknesses were stated in the A19 record and are the questions here: (1) ADAPT contains a rollout while CLAMP does not, so the gain over CLAMP mixes learning and rollout; (2) the true scale was always inside the prior and one kill showed the scale, which favours the learner; (3) the error was one common factor; (4) no decision time was measured.
Looks before this plan (not used as results): the printed A19 outcome (all of it); timing runs and an informativeness check on the exploratory seed 3 (5 instances per cell at n = 5, 16 cells per condition; informative cells 6 of 16 for the dwell (6, 12), 6 for (3, 6), 6 for (8, 16), 8 for (4, 12) and 4 for (6, 8)); the audit run (seed 3, 64 rows, 16 cells), whose printed output I saw: its regret figures at the condition (3, 6) showed ADAPT well above NOM and well below the informed planner, from 1 to 3 instances; nothing is inferred from that.
Error found by that check: the A19 learner raised an IndexError when no scale of the prior is consistent with the observation (true dwell outside the prior), because the progress of an alive threat was not clamped to the largest dwell of the scale model. A19 code stays unchanged; A20 overrides one method (Learner20.state_in clamps that progress). The clamp changes nothing for a scale with positive posterior weight, and for a true scale inside the prior no observation is impossible, so the A19 results are not affected (argued, and tested in A20 on the three in-prior scales by comparing the A19 and A20 values on three instances each; not re-run on the A19 main seed).
Expectation stated before the run [OPEN]: ROBUST-LEARNER is not expected; USEFUL is plausible. At the dwell (3, 6) and (8, 16) a kill after 6 or 8 steps is read as the scale 1.2 or 0.8 and misleads the learner, and the same holds for the class-specific dwell (4, 12) and (6, 8); Q2 may fail in some of the eight statements. Q1 is likely at scale 0.8 (where CLAMP is identical to NOM), uncertain at 1.2 and 1.4, where CLAMP with a rollout may be close to ADAPT.

**A20.2 Methods (src/dwell/a20.py).** As in A19: NOM, CLAMP, ADAPT, ADAPT_V, informed CEPS_T and RU_CEP_T, at n = 5 also the exact goal optimum U_opt_T. New: RU_CLAMP, the one-step goal rollout of the nominal CEPS applied to the clamped nominal state (progress of an alive threat clamped to D0[-1] - 1; no learning); at the control it equals the nominal rollout RU_CEP. Every row also has the mean time per policy call in ms and the number of calls per method (rollout values are memoised within the instance, so this is a warm per-instance figure). The prior support of the learner is the four scales of A19 with equal probability.

**A20.3 Design.** Eval seed 20261311 (not used before), bootstrap seed 20261313. True dwell conditions (name: dwell of the two classes): 1.0 control (5, 10); in the prior 0.8 (4, 8), 1.2 (6, 12), 1.4 (7, 14); outside the prior 0.6 (3, 6) and 1.6 (8, 16); class specific SP (4, 12) and CP (6, 8). Base instance family of A14 to A19; deadlines, values, slew, labels and priors are those of the nominal instance. Cells: rho in {1.1, 1.5}, s in {2, 4}, label accuracy in {0.5, 0.9}, lam in {0.25, 0.5} (16 per condition and size). Sizes: n = 5 (exact level, 50 instances per cell) and n = 7 (level ru, 40 instances per cell); 256 cells and 11520 rows. No pilot: the design extends A19, which had a pilot. Informative cell: the mean survival probability of the informed planner CEPS_T lies in [0.10, 0.99], chosen per condition and size.

**A20.4 Claims and labels (fixed now).**
- Q0(n), control, n = 5 and 7: the one-sided 95% upper bound of the mean of U_RU_CLAMP minus U_ADAPT is at most 0.03 (learning costs little against the non-learning rollout planner when the nominal model is right).
- Q1(cond, n), cond in {0.8, 1.2, 1.4}, n in {5, 7} (six statements): the lower bound of the mean of U_ADAPT minus U_RU_CLAMP is at least 0.01 (learning adds to not abandoning plus a rollout).
- Q2(cond, n), cond in {0.6, 1.6, SP, CP}, n in {5, 7} (eight statements): the lower bound of the mean of U_ADAPT minus U_NOM is at least 0.03 (the learner still beats the nominal planner outside its prior or with a class-specific error).
- Q3 (descriptive, all 14 non-control statements): ADAPT against RU_CLAMP: LEARNING-BETTER if the lower bound is at least 0.01, CLAMP-BETTER if the upper bound is at most -0.01, else MIXED.
- Label, first match: NO OUTCOME (a Q0 or Q1 statement has no informative cell, or fewer than 6 of the 8 Q2 statements have one); ROBUST-LEARNER (Q1 in at least 5 of 6, Q0 in 2 of 2 and Q2 in every defined Q2 statement); USEFUL (Q2 in at least 5 statements and Q0 in 2 of 2); LIMITED (Q2 in at least 1); NO GAIN. A statement without an informative cell is UNDEFINED and does not count as holding.
- Second label: LEARNING-NEEDED if Q1 holds in at least 5 of 6, else CLAMP-ROLLOUT-ENOUGH.
- Reported, not part of the label: ADAPT against CLAMP and against the informed rollout planner, the mean regret of every method against U_opt_T at n = 5, the time per policy call by method and size.

**A20.5 Statistics.** As in A16 to A19: instance-level bootstrap stratified by cell, 2000 resamples, one-sided 95% bounds, pooled over the informative cells of a condition and size. The label is a conjunction of bounds; no multiplicity correction is applied across statements or amendments.

**A20.8 What each label means (not commitments).** ROBUST-LEARNER: the learner beats the nominal planner even outside its prior and with class-specific error, and adds to a non-learning rollout planner. USEFUL: it beats the nominal planner in most misspecified conditions; the failing statements are reported as limits. LIMITED or NO GAIN: the learner works only when the true scale is in its prior, and the paper says so. LEARNING-NEEDED against CLAMP-ROLLOUT-ENOUGH says whether learning is needed once a planner does not abandon and uses a rollout.
**A20.9 Limits stated now.** n = 5 and 7 only and no exact reference at n = 7; the four extra conditions are my choice and not a model of real dwell error; the fallback of the learner when no scale is consistent is the uniform prior, not tuned; no reinforcement learning or Bayes-optimal learner; thresholds are mine; informative cells are selected with the informed planner and differ between conditions; the times are warm per instance and measured while four workers share the laptop, so only the ratios between planners are meaningful; the amendments A1 to A20 are post-hoc relative to each other.
