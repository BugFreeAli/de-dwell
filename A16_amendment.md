### A16 (2026-11-23): planner-side confirmatory evaluation and a regime variable (plan written before any A16 code is committed)
Status: plan only. The freeze point is the commit that adds this text (workflow of 2026-11-17: no tags). No A16 run has been made on the author's machine when this text is committed.

**A16.1 Why, and what is already known (disclosure).** A14 and A14.r1 showed, for the survival objective, that the exact expected-value optimum is mismatched (up to 32% to 38% of the survival probability at n = 4 and up to 19% at n = 5). A15 (evaluation seed 20261261) gave descriptive pooled differences at larger sizes: the survival-aware certainty-equivalent planner CEPS minus its expected-value counterpart CEP was +3.2, +6.4 and +7.3 points of survival probability at n = 5, 7 and 9, and RU_CEP minus CEPS +1.6, +1.8 and +2.4 points; no paired intervals were computed and those differences were not planned contrasts. A16 tests these claims on a fresh seed with intervals, and tests one explanatory variable of the size of the mismatch. Learned scorers are out of scope (A15: LEARN-BEHIND).
Exploratory looks that fixed the regime variable and its band (post-hoc, by me in my sandbox on throwaway seeds, not repeated on the author's machine): (1) seed 31, n = 7, 16 cells of the A15 grid, 30 instances each (480 instances), planners CEP and CEPS only: the gain of CEPS over CEP by goal tightness tau = need / E[killed value of CEP] was 0.6 points for tau <= 0.8 (124 instances), 6.8 points for 0.8 < tau <= 0.9 (58), 7.5 for 0.9 to 1.0 (68), 11.4 for 1.0 to 1.1 (54), 3.4 for 1.1 to 1.25 (69) and about 0 above 1.25 (107); overall 3.8 points. The band 0.8 <= tau <= 1.1 was chosen from this. (2) seed 33, tiny (n = 7, 9, 11 with 10, 6 and 3 instances per cell; 90, 54 and 18 informative instances): CEPS minus CEP +7.1, +6.0 and +12.2 points; RU_CEP minus CEPS +2.0 (upper bound 3.3) at n = 7 and +1.0 (upper bound 1.8) at n = 9; in-band gain 8.5 against 4.5 points outside (n = 7 and 9 pooled, 144 instances). The seeds 31 and 33 are not used again.
Expectation stated before the run [OPEN]: P1 is likely to hold; P2 and P3 are uncertain (the second look suggests a smaller regime difference, about 4 points, than the first, and an upper bound for RU_CEP minus CEPS close to 3 points at n = 7); the outcome PARTIAL is plausible.

**A16.2 Claims and thresholds (fixed now).** Informative cell: mean survival probability of the reference method in [0.10, 0.99]; the reference is RU_CEP, or CEPS where RU_CEP is not run (n = 11).
- P1 (survival-aware planning pays): the one-sided 95% lower bound of the mean of U_CEPS minus U_CEP is at least 0.01 at each of n = 5, 7 and 9.
- P2 (the cheap planner is adequate): the one-sided 95% upper bound of the mean of U_RU_CEP minus U_CEPS is at most 0.03 at n = 7 and at n = 9.
- P3 (regime variable): over the informative instances of n = 5, 7, 9, the mean gain U_CEPS minus U_CEP of the instances with 0.8 <= tau <= 1.1 minus that of all other instances has a one-sided 95% lower bound of at least 0.03; tau = need / E[killed value of CEP] (exact expectation, enumerating all hidden-class vectors; tau is infinite if CEP kills nothing).
- Outcome, first match: NO OUTCOME (a size has no informative cell); CONFIRMED (P1, P2 and P3 all hold); PARTIAL (P1 holds at every size but P2 or P3 fails); NOT CONFIRMED (otherwise).
The joint claim is an intersection of the individual claims, so no correction across claims is applied; every claim is also reported separately.

**A16.3 Design.** Eval seed 20261271 (not used before), bootstrap seed 20261272. Cells: rho in {1.1, 1.5}, s in {2, 4}, label accuracy in {0.5, 0.9}, lam in {0.25, 0.5} (16 cells per size; instances, hidden classes, goal and exact evaluation as in A14 and A15). Sizes and levels: n = 5, 60 instances per cell, level exact (CEP, CEPS, RU_CEP, exact goal optimum, DPEV, RU_DPEV, exact expected-value optimum); n = 7, 80 instances, level ru (CEP, CEPS, RU_CEP); n = 9, 50 instances, level ru; n = 11, 30 instances, level cheap (CEP and CEPS only; descriptive). Robustness variants (descriptive), n = 5, label accuracy 0.5, rho in {1.1, 1.5}, s in {2, 4}, lam in {0.25, 0.5}, 40 instances per cell, level exact: K3 (three hardness classes, D = (3, 6, 10)), wide (D = (3, 12)), equal (all values 2). Every method is evaluated exactly (no sampling).
**A16.4 Statistics.** Instance-level bootstrap stratified by cell (2000 resamples), pooled mean over the informative cells of a size, one-sided 95% bounds (5th and 95th percentile). Deterministic planners on shared instances, so the unit is the instance within its cell; the intervals condition on the instance generator and on the grid. Everything not listed in A16.2 is descriptive and carries no threshold: n = 11; the exact regret of every method at n = 5; the gain by tau bin; the Spearman correlation of |tau - 1| with the gain and, at n = 5, of |tau_ev - 1| with the DPEV loss (tau_ev with the exact expected-value optimum); and, for each variant, CEPS minus CEP with its lower bound and the regrets of CEP, CEPS, RU_CEP, DPEV and RU_DPEV against the exact optimum (a variant is said to show the mismatch if the lower bound of CEPS minus CEP is at least 0.01 in its informative cells; no other claim is made).
**A16.5 Verification before any main run.** tests/test_a16.py and tests/test_a16_analysis.py (18 tests): expected killed value equals the exact expected-value optimum for the optimal policy and the known value on a deterministic instance; row invariants and columns by level; variants use their own instance parameters; a method above the optimum is flagged; informative cells and the choice of the reference method; stratified bootstrap (deterministic, ordered); sign of the differences; band limits; the outcomes CONFIRMED, PARTIAL, NOT CONFIRMED and NO OUTCOME; P1 uses the lower bound, P2 the upper bound, P3 the lower bound. scripts/mutate_a16.py: 17 mutations, all caught. Audit on an exploratory seed with the same code. scripts/a16_gate.py writes results/a16_gate.json with the SHA-256 of eight code files (a16.py, analysis_a16.py, m0u.py, m0.py, dp.py, rules.py, instances.py, run_a16.py); run_a16.py refuses the role main without a gate that carries the current hash.

**A16.7 What each outcome means (not commitments).** CONFIRMED: the paper's central claim is established on fresh seeds (expected-value planning is mismatched for the survival objective; a cheap survival-aware planner repairs most of it; the mismatch is largest when the goal is tight). PARTIAL: P1 stands and the missing part is reported as the limit (RU_CEP adds more than three points, or the regime variable does not separate). NOT CONFIRMED: the A15 pattern was not reproduced and the contribution must be reduced to the exact small-n results.
**A16.8 Limits stated now.** Planners only; CEPS and RU_CEP are those of A14.4; n <= 9 for the primary claims; the band of tau and the thresholds are my choices fixed after exploratory looks (A16.1); informative cells are selected with RU_CEP, a method of the comparison; intervals do not include variation of the generator or the grid; n = 11 and the variants are descriptive with few instances; the amendments A1 to A16 are post-hoc relative to each other and no multiplicity correction is applied across amendments.
