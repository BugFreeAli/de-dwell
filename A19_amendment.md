### A19 (2026-11-27): learning the dwell scale during the episode (plan written before any A19 code is committed)
Status: plan only. The freeze point is the commit that adds this text (workflow of 2026-11-17: no tags). No A19 pilot or main run has been made when this text is committed.

**A19.1 Why, and what is already known (disclosure).** A18 (outcome MOSTLY) showed that a wrong dwell scale costs the nominal survival-aware planner 12 to 54 points of survival probability against the same planner built on the true dwell, and that the informed expected-value planner CEP_T beats the nominal CEPS in all nine statements. The loss was measured for a planner that abandons a threat which survives more dwell than the largest nominal dwell, and that never learns the scale. A19 asks whether a planner that learns the scale from what it observes in the episode recovers the loss, and whether the cheap fix of not abandoning (CLAMP) already does most of it.
Looks before this plan (not used as results): the printed A18 outcome (all of it); timing runs (seed 3); the audit run (seed 3, 40 rows, 8 cells, rho 1.5, s 2, a 0.5, lam 0.25), whose printed output I saw: in its one informative cell per size at scale 0.8 ADAPT was as good as the informed rollout planner and far above NOM, with 6 and 4 instances; nothing is inferred from that. Design choices made after seeing A18: the four scales as the support of the prior, the baselines CLAMP and ADAPT_V, the reference RU_CEP_T, the thresholds below (the 0.03 and 0.01 margins are those of A16 to A18; the recovery limits 0.5 and 0.2 and the pilot rule are new and mine).
Expectation stated before the run [OPEN]: a kill shows the scale exactly (the eight dwell values 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12 and 14 are distinct), so ADAPT should recover a large part of the gap at the scales 1.2 and 1.4 and at 0.8; what is lost before the first kill and at n = 7 is uncertain; P2 (little loss when the nominal model is right) is likely to hold; CLAMP may already recover much of the loss at 1.2 and 1.4, because the abandon rule is a main cause of the A18 loss; WORKS is plausible but not certain.

**A19.2 Model and planners.** The world is that of A18: class k needs scale x D0[k] dwell steps, with D0 = (5, 10); deadlines, values, slew, labels and priors are those of the nominal instance; the same instance index gives the same instance at every scale. The planners know that the scale is one of SUPPORT = (0.8, 1.0, 1.2, 1.4) with equal prior probability, and the true scale is always one of them. They see time, current target, the dwell given to each threat and which threats were killed. Threats are independent given the scale, so the posterior of the scale is proportional to the prior times, over the threats, the probability of what was seen: for an alive threat with x dwell steps, the prior mass of the classes whose dwell under that scale exceeds x; for a threat killed after x steps, the mass of the classes whose dwell equals x. Methods (src/dwell/a19.py), all evaluated exactly in the true world:
- NOM: the nominal CEPS of A18 (reproduces its values).
- CLAMP: CEPS on the nominal model with the progress of an alive threat clamped to D0[-1] - 1, so that a threat is never abandoned for having survived too much dwell; no learning.
- ADAPT: posterior-weighted one-step lookahead: for every scale with positive posterior weight, the exact goal probability of each action followed by CEPS of that scale (the rollout of A14), weighted by the posterior; the best action is taken.
- ADAPT_V: posterior-weighted vote of the CEPS actions of the scales (no rollout; the cheap learner).
- Informed references (built on the true model and evaluated in it): CEPS_T and RU_CEP_T (the one-step goal rollout of CEPS_T); at n = 5 also the exact goal optimum U_opt_T. No learner can exceed U_opt_T.

**A19.3 Design.** Pilot first, then main. Pilot (role pilot): eval seed 20261302, n = 5, 20 instances per cell, 64 cells and 1280 rows, descriptive, with the go or stop rule of A19.4. Main (role main, run only after a pilot GO): eval seed 20261301 (not used before), bootstrap seed 20261303, sizes n = 5 (exact level) and n = 7 (level ru), 60 instances per cell, 128 cells and 7680 rows. Cells: true scale in {1.0 (control), 0.8, 1.2, 1.4} x rho in {1.1, 1.5}, s in {2, 4}, label accuracy in {0.5, 0.9}, lam in {0.25, 0.5}. Base instance family of A14 to A18. n = 9 is not run: ADAPT costs four rollouts per decision and n = 9 took about two hours in A18. Informative cell: the mean survival probability of the informed planner CEPS_T lies in [0.10, 0.99]; it is chosen per scale and size.

**A19.4 Claims and labels (fixed now).** Statements per true scale in {0.8, 1.2, 1.4} and size n in {5, 7}:
- P1(scale, n) (six statements): the one-sided 95% lower bound of the mean of U_ADAPT minus U_NOM is at least 0.03.
- P2(n) (two statements, at true scale 1.0): the one-sided 95% upper bound of the mean of U_NOM minus U_ADAPT is at most 0.03.
- Recovery R: the mean of U_ADAPT minus U_NOM divided by the mean of U_RU_CEP_T minus U_NOM (the share of the gap to the informed rollout planner that ADAPT closes), with the same resampled instances in numerator and denominator; per statement (class HIGH if the lower bound is at least 0.5, SOME if at least 0.2, else LOW; UNDEFINED if the mean gap is below 0.01) and pooled over the six statements.
- Label, first match: NO OUTCOME (a P1 or P2 statement has no informative cell); WORKS (P1 in 6 of 6, P2 in 2 of 2 and the pooled recovery lower bound at least 0.5); HELPS (P1 in at least 4 of 6 and P2 in 2 of 2); LIMITED (P1 in at least 1); NO GAIN.
- Second label: S1(scale, n) is the lower bound of the mean of U_ADAPT minus U_CLAMP at least 0.01; LEARNING-ADDS if S1 holds in at least 5 of 6, else CLAMP-COMPARABLE (learning adds little to not abandoning).
- Pilot rule (n = 5, descriptive, not a claim): GO if the pooled point recovery over the scales 0.8, 1.2 and 1.4 is at least 0.20 and the mean of U_NOM minus U_ADAPT at the control is at most 0.05; otherwise STOP and the paper reports A18 with CLAMP and the pilot as limits. The pilot instances are not reused in the main run.
Reported, not part of the label: U_ADAPT_V against U_ADAPT; the mean regret of every method against U_opt_T at n = 5; everything else is descriptive.

**A19.5 Statistics.** As in A16 to A18: instance-level bootstrap stratified by cell, 2000 resamples, one-sided 95% bounds, pooled over the informative cells of a scale and size. The label is a conjunction of bounds; no multiplicity correction is applied across statements or amendments.

**A19.6 Verification before any run.** tests/test_a19.py (32 tests): the posterior (no information, alive threats, each of the eight kill dwell values shows its scale, impossible observation falls back to the prior, the truth is never excluded along the evaluated histories); hand case in which NOM abandons, CLAMP continues and ADAPT learns; with a single-scale support ADAPT equals the informed rollout planner and ADAPT_V the informed CEPS; NOM reproduces the values of A18; the decision cache does not change the probability (independent uncached implementation); the weighting of the posterior in ADAPT and ADAPT_V (stubbed scale models); the support of the configuration reaches the learner; rows without violation (never above the true optimum, no kill after its deadline); informed references recomputed independently; analysis (bounds instead of point estimates, informed reference selects the cells, labels, clamp class, recovery with paired resampling, pilot rule, regret table); job layout; gate and seed rules for audit, pilot and main. scripts/mutate_a19.py: 39 mutations, all caught. Audit on an exploratory seed (configs/a19_audit.json, seed 3). scripts/a19_gate.py writes results/a19_gate.json with the SHA-256 of ten code files (a19.py, a18.py, analysis_a16.py, analysis_a19.py, m0u.py, m0.py, dp.py, rules.py, instances.py, run_a19.py); run_a19.py refuses the roles pilot and main without a gate that carries the current hash and refuses the audit role with the pilot or main seed.

**A19.8 What each label means (not commitments).** WORKS: a planner that learns the dwell scale repairs most of the A18 loss without a cost when the nominal model is right, and the paper's survival claim holds under dwell error. HELPS: it repairs part of it; the shortfall is reported with the statements that fail. LIMITED or NO GAIN: learning from the kills in an episode does not repair the loss and the paper reports A18 as a limitation. LEARNING-ADDS against CLAMP-COMPARABLE says whether learning is needed or not abandoning is enough.
**A19.9 Limits stated now.** The true scale is always in the support of the prior (a scale outside it is not tested); one common multiplicative error for all classes, fixed per episode; four scales and the base family only; n = 5 and 7 only and no exact reference at n = 7; informative cells are selected with the informed planner and differ between scales; ADAPT is a one-step lookahead with a CEPS base policy (not the Bayes-optimal learner, whose exact value is not computed); thresholds are mine; the amendments A1 to A19 are post-hoc relative to each other.
